A3VR Hybrid 1.14.0-alpha.1

- Increased motion-aim response and smooth-turn speed.
- Added progressive stand/crouch/prone cycling on the right stick.
- Added physical crouch detection from headset height.
- Added vertical camera/turret control in vehicles.
- Improved door and native interaction input.
- Prevented stale UI state from disabling movement or motion aiming.
- Restored the sharp wide-FOV profile: 4K internal rendering with 1080p borderless output.
- Restored the approved comfort-mono scale; moderate black borders are expected.
- Fixed automatic startup from Workshop paths containing square brackets.
- Public builds now follow the active system OpenXR runtime instead of forcing Meta OpenXR.

This remains an experimental comfort-mono bridge rather than native per-eye stereo VR. Disable BattlEye, do not use it on protected multiplayer servers, and keep keyboard/mouse available for Arma's contextual controls.
