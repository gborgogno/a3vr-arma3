A3VR Hybrid is an experimental OpenXR bridge for Arma 3.

Current features:
- Stable VR presentation with 6DoF head tracking
- Right-hand motion-controller aiming
- VR controller buttons and analog movement
- Smooth turning
- Reversible high-quality VR graphics preset
- Compatibility with additional Arma 3 mods through the official Launcher

Version: 1.13.0 experimental

Important:
- Intended for local/private testing.
- Requires an active OpenXR headset and 64-bit Windows.
- Do not load an older A3VR build at the same time.
- Native DLL injection is required for D3D11 frame capture. Use without BattlEye and do not join protected multiplayer servers.
- This is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with Bohemia Interactive.

The Quality profile captures Arma at 2560x1440, improves shadow distance and terrain/object detail, and backs up the original Arma settings before changing them.
